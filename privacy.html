Privacy Policy

Brand Mention Monitor (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a consent-based service for authorized businesses to review permitted social-media content related to their brands.

Information we collect

We may collect account details, organization details, brand names and aliases, platform account identifiers, access tokens, content links, captions, comments, review decisions, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, connect approved social accounts, find likely brand mentions, display evidence for human review, provide notifications, maintain security, and improve reliability. We do not use connected content to publish on a user's behalf unless a separate permission and feature are explicitly enabled.

Platform data

Social-platform data is accessed only through approved permissions, authorized connections, or permitted providers. We follow the applicable platform terms and retain data only for the purposes agreed with the customer.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use carefully selected infrastructure and service providers to host, secure, analyze, or deliver the service. They may process information only to provide services to us and under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Retention and deletion

Customers may request deletion of their organization, connections, and stored evidence. We remove access tokens when a connection is revoked and delete data according to the customer's retention settings, subject to legal or security requirements.

Security

We use access controls, encryption where appropriate, server-side secret storage, audit logging, and least-privilege permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of information associated with your organization. Contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
