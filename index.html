Evidence before action.

Verify the evidence

Built for responsible monitoring

Useful context without automatic publishing.

Brand Mention Monitor does not automatically publish content, contact users, or moderate conversations. It is designed to support customer-care, communications, and reputation teams with reviewable evidence.

Data principles

Clear permissions. Minimal data. Accountable review.

  • Access only follows the permissions granted by an account or business.
  • Credentials are kept server-side and are not exposed in browser code.
  • Results are suggestions for human review, not claims of fact.
  • Users can request access changes or deletion.
